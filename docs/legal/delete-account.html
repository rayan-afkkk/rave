Delete your Rave account

In the app

  1. Open Rave and tap your profile, then Settings.
  2. Scroll to Data and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If asked, sign in again and repeat.

Without the app

Email support@example.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, activity summaries, shared routes in your friends’ feed, habit backup, friends, friend requests, nudges, invites, league entries, shared streaks and competition entries. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall Rave.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google’s own policy. Copies in Google’s backup systems are removed within their standard backup cycle.
